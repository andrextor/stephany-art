The great tree is a door to another world, where the river is made of fairy dust and a thousand golden fireflies glow in the turquoise forest. A sorceress of shadow and wind, with a beacon of light, crosses the veil of a drowsy kingdom. And she understands, looking around, that magic is her eternal return.
