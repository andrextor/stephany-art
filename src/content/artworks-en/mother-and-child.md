And suddenly atypical motherhood knocks on your door, and everything you thought you knew about being a mother fades away. You walk an unknown, dark path…

Guided by a tiny, peculiar blue light that helps you move forward, taking strong steps of love and struggle. A path that at times becomes a labyrinth of uncertainty, fear, sadness and helplessness.

The greatest goal is that this little light never goes out: you protect it, you hold it, you sing to it. You even build it a little glass box so that nothing and no one can put it out. So that it grows as big and happy as it can.

But what happens when the glass breaks and the shards fly everywhere?

A gust of fears floods your world. Fears of what comes from outside, of a world that doesn't understand; the blue light seems strange to them. Looks of ignorance appear, of curiosity, prejudice, indifference, pity, incomprehension.

Some shards manage to wound you, but you keep going, so broken yet so strong, so vulnerable yet with so much strength.

After the crash comes the calm. You manage to silence the noise of outside perceptions and judgments, and you realize the blue light is still so alive, so radiant, a little frightened, but your arms calm it; they are its refuge.

You fill yourself with courage and you keep walking together, making your way despite so many rocks and obstacles.

Nothing stops you. The blue light keeps growing and shines brighter every day. And you, so in love, so proud.

Some nights you allow yourself to feel. You scream in silence; you hear the scream inside, so deafening, and sadness becomes your companion.

Then you heal your wounds, ignore the looks, turn all the voices you hear during the day down to zero, and finally you manage to sleep.

The next day you look at the enigmatic, mysterious and wonderful light that came to keep you company, you hold it, and you know that your infinite love will now be its guide.
