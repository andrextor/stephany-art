Between the waves and the firmament stretches a luminous path that wraps us in eternity.

Every brushstroke on this canvas is a bridge woven over the silence of your absence. The intimate pulse that lets me hold your hand even when the path turns invisible.

You left in the springtime of life, toward the infinite clarity, toward that serene rest that shelters you today and floods us with peace.

Yet your absence remains, an unbreakable compass guiding my steps every dawn.

And though the pain of parting sometimes clouds the horizon, I know you dwell in the highest, purest radiance of the infinite, sowing stars in the sky.

Until my own steps reach the threshold where the light gives me back your embrace.
